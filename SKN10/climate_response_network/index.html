How ranking works

Candidate health facilities are drawn from OpenStreetMap. The tool attempts to compare road-network routes using OSRM and ranks reachable facilities by estimated route duration, then applies a simple demand-to-unit planning indicator.

Data honesty
  • Road route estimates are not live traffic.
  • Flooded/closed roads are not known unless an authorized current dataset is supplied.
  • Hospital beds, ICU availability and capacity are not available in this demo.
